Description

David Reynolds is delighted to bring to market this beautifully presented 4-bedroom semi-detached residence extending to c. 120m², ideally positioned on an elevated site in the residential area of Cartron, Sligo.

Finished in a fresh, contemporary style and presented in turn-key condition, this impressive home offers bright, spacious and beautifully maintained accommodation, making it an ideal choice for families, first-time buyers or those seeking a quality home within easy reach of Sligo Town.

A particular highlight of the property is its wonderful outlook over Sligo Bay towards the iconic Benbulben, providing an impressive backdrop to this superb home.

Internally, the property combines modern finishes with comfortable family living. Features include a welcoming living room with open fireplace, fitted Sliderobes, a bright conservatory, and oil-fired central heating with a combi boiler.

The property also benefits from a high B energy rating, offering excellent energy efficiency and comfort.

The location is equally impressive. Cartron is an established and popular residential neighbourhood, conveniently situated within minutes` walk of a host of local services and amenities, while Sligo Town Centre is also within easy reach.
